# eternal:contrast-01 — one accent carries the composition

**Rule:** use a single accent hue against a neutral field; let saturation and
weight, not additional hues, create emphasis. Add a second accent only when the
composition has two genuinely independent messages.

**Check:** count distinct saturated hues in the piece. More than two → justify
or cut.

**Source:** Eternal palette-choice signals (see `ex-0001`); players chose a
one-accent frame 4:1 over multi-hue splits.
